# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Job seekers, software engineers, and professionals applying for tech and international opportunities in the DRC and remotely.

## Product Purpose

Automate and streamline the personal job search pipeline. JobPilot aggregates jobs from both local DRC job boards and international platforms, scores job matches against the candidate's resume using multi-provider AI engines (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI), extracts concise executive summaries for profiles, and drafts custom cover letters.

## Positioning

The only personal job search automation platform combining local DRC job board scraping (ReliefWeb, Emploi.cd, CongoJob, UNJobs) with global platforms (Greenhouse, Lever, RemoteOK, Ashby) and multi-LLM fit scoring with tailored resume/cover letter generation.

## Operating Context

Daily job hunting workflow: checking newly ingested jobs, filtering by status (NEW, SCORED, APPLIED, INTERVIEWING, REJECTED, OFFER) and source platform, scoring match quality against candidate background, uploading/reformatting resume summaries, and generating custom cover letters.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Multi-Source Job Aggregation**: ReliefWeb, Emploi.cd, CongoJob, UNJobs, Greenhouse, RemoteOK, Lever, Ashby, and manual entry.
- **Multi-LLM Fit Scoring**: Gemini (default engine), Claude AI, OpenAI GPT-4.
- **Executive Summary Extraction**: Automated parsing of resume uploads into concise 2–3 sentence summaries.
- **Tailored Resume & Cover Letter Generation**: Dynamic prompt directives tailored to candidate skills and targeted job descriptions.
- **Theme & Aesthetic**: Dark/Light mode support, curated HSL color accents, dot grid background patterns, glassmorphism cards.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: JobPilot
- **Tone**: Tactical, empowering, crisp, high-density dashboard UX.
- **Default AI Engine**: Gemini.

## Product Principles

1. **Local-First & Global Ready**: Prioritize relevant local DRC opportunities while providing seamless access to international remote jobs.
2. **Speed & High Density**: Display maximum relevant job metadata, match scores, and action triggers with zero fluff.
3. **Candidate Control**: Let candidates choose their preferred AI scoring engine and refine their resume/profile at any time.
4. **Instant Navigations**: Ensure instant static shell rendering (`◐`) and smooth dynamic streaming across all routes.
